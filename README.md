# PackReport
PackReport is a mod that gives a plain-text report of your modpack, thus making it easy to copy and share information about your installed mods with friends and such.

It has copy buttons on the main menu and pause menu that can be moved that allow you to copy the report without sifting through your files like an unc. The moving pause-menu button can also remember its position between sessions.

The report contains ifnormation about your instance and what mods you have, along with metadata

Features

    Creates a plain-text report of installed mods and available metadata that can be copied
    Has information about your Minecraft instance
    Copies the report to your clipboard with a click.
    Has a button on both the main menu and pause menu.
    It allows you to change the position of the pause-menu button, with its position saved between sessions.
    Works locally without requiring an online account or cloud service to generate the report.

PackReport was made to make sharing modpack information simpler when asking for help, documenting a setup, or providing details about installed mods.
